# M27 Completion Report

M27 completed on 25 September 2026.

## Outcome

The approved M22 no-scroll console now integrates M23 enterprise configuration, M24 change governance, M25 information assurance and M26 case intelligence through a movable Operational Assurance workspace. Model 1 remains a complete enterprise-operated system. Model 2 adds a separate consultant control centre and client decision view.

The display keeps risk severity, evidence confidence, workflow status and calculated resilience separate. Enterprise, consultant and client views require different scopes. The visualization and simulation layers remain read-only with zero live actions.

## Controlled pilots

Two immutable, tenant-bound pilot records prove the required sequences:

- enterprise: configuration, live observation, private simulation, approval, information escalation, outcome review and lesson publication;
- consultant: assignment, authorised ingestion, expert assessment, private simulation, client approval, sandbox execution, outcome validation and lesson publication.

Both pilots require passing usability, accessibility, performance, security, retention, audit and tenant-isolation checks. Incomplete or failed evidence cannot be recorded as a passing pilot.
